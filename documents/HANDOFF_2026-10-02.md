Three programs Andre named, to research in depth — what they did, how they were funded, outcomes:

1. **Constitutional "special position" recognition** — government obligated by law to protect and promote Malays' educational, economic, political, religious, social, and cultural interests.
2. **Subsidized/free education for qualifying Malays** — free education for Malays who qualified for school/college/university admission, reducing financial barriers while keeping merit requirements (per a 1965 Lee Kuan Yew speech).
3. **MENDAKI** — Malay/Muslim-led organization launched 1982 providing subsidized tutoring; grew from 6 tuition centers / 880 students to 16 centers / 4,450 students by 1989.
